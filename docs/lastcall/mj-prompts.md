# Last Call — Midjourney key frames

Look references for Event 16. The new set pieces are modelled in Blender from these frames
(`tools/blender/lastcall_kit.py`), the same way Calder Basin was built from `docs/calder-ref`.
Frames are saved here as `docs/lastcall/ref/NN_name.jpg`.

Shared look (every prompt ends with it): 1:45 AM, wet asphalt, sodium and LED light, haze, cinematic, AFTERHOURS night.

```
SUFFIX = cinematic night street racing game environment, unreal engine 5 quality, wet asphalt reflections, volumetric haze, anamorphic lens, high detail, no people --ar 16:9 --style raw
```

Read-off for the kit:
- 01 Halo tower: a squat glass drum (~60 m tall, ~45 m across) on a dark plinth, horizontal magenta bands with a cyan band, the loop's helix visible through the glass, cranes and the lit bridge behind.
- 02 Halo interior: glass-and-rib vault, the road edged with continuous magenta strips on both barriers, cyan rim light.
- 03 The Ring: a single steel loop standing on two raked A-pylons with a cable fan, white LED on the inner edge, ember on the outer edge, skyline behind.
- 04 The Ring from the Boulevard: a clean orange ring of light at the end of the straight; sodium lamps, palms/trees in the median.
- 05 Portal: a white shell hood leaning over the mouth, inset cyan-white LED ring, the Battleship on the left.
- 06 Bore: round corrugated liner, teal work lights at the crown, amber lamps along the walls, standing water.
- 07 Franklin Square: round fountain basin lit gold, carousel with a bulb canopy, festoon lights in old trees, curved wet paths.
- 08 Valley Green: low stone arch bridge, stone inn with warm windows behind, mist on the creek, gravel path.
- 09 Express trench: concrete walls with amber wall lamps, overpass with green signs, poles with cameras.
- 10 I-95: white T-piers in the river, orange sodium on the deck, pink casino tower.
- 11 Battleship: grey hull uplit from the quay, lit superstructure.
- 12 Gantry: dark steel truss, orange SHORTCUT arrow box, yellow-black chevron cushion on the gore.
- 13 Parkway: double rows of flags, golden museum on the hill, fountain jets lit cyan.
- 14 Bridge: blue steel trusses, white LED beads along the main cables.
