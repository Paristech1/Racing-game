Last Call

12.24 KM LAP · 3 LAPS · 36.7 KM RACE · 2 LOOPS · 4 SHORTCUTS · 95 M OF CLIMB · 17 CORNERS · TIGHTEST R 24 M

The Bridge Run, Harbor Line, Roosevelt Blvd and Mt Airy joined into one clockwise lap of the city at 1:45 AM. Fast and technical sectors alternate, and each loop sits at the end of a long straight, so you see it coming.

The Halo

57 M TALL · 260 M OF ROAD · KM 4.7 · INSIDE THE PORT RICHMOND VENT TOWER

The tunnel climbs out from under the river into a glass and steel ventilation shaft, and the road goes up and over inside it. From outside the tower glows like a lantern on the waterfront.

Overdrive gem on the crown.

The Ring

66 M TALL · 300 M OF ROAD · KM 7.5 · HUNTING PARK, END OF THE BOULEVARD

An open-air steel loop on twin pylons, lit edge to edge. It stands on the line of the Boulevard, so it is in front of you for the whole mile.

Slingshot on the crown.

Both loops ease in and out (the pitch rate builds from zero, peaks at the crown, and fades), so there is no jolt at the bottom. Each exit runs 17 m to the side of its entry, and you pass under the loop you just drove. Gravity is real: you lose speed climbing and get it back coming down.
